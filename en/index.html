Understand what is changing in the health of someone you care for

MAR VITAL brings together a person's measurements, wearable data, trends, alerts and history, and puts them where their family can read them.

It runs on HealthOS, the platform that collects the data, organises it per person and explains it in plain words. It does not diagnose and does not replace professional medical care.

How it works Privacy

How it works

Three steps, and none of them invents anything that was not measured.

Collect

Measurements entered by the family, data from an authorised watch or band, messages, photos and documents. Every data point keeps its origin: who or what provided it.

Understand

Series are drawn with their real gaps — if nothing was measured, the line breaks — alongside text that describes what was recorded: what is visible, what changed and what is worth watching.

Follow up

Alerts are opened by deterministic rules and show the data that triggered them. The family sees what happened, when, and why the system flags it.

Capabilities

What the system does today, stated plainly.

Wearable data

Heart rate, oxygen, steps, sleep and other measures the device actually delivers. What a device does not measure is reported as not measured.

Trends

Charts over time ranges, with the person's own usual baseline when there are enough of their measurements to compute it.

Alerts

Deterministic rules, with the evidence in plain sight and its level. An alert flags; it does not diagnose and does not contact anyone on its own.

Timeline

Measurements, messages, voice notes, photos, documents, alerts, medication and appointments, in order and with their provenance.

Family care

Several people can follow the same patient, each with the permissions they were granted.

Communication

Messages and voice notes arriving through an authorised channel, attached to the right person.

Built around the person

Each patient has their own profile, and the system looks at one at a time.

On entering you choose who you are looking at, and the whole screen — summary, charts, alerts, history — belongs to that person and to no one else. Switching profile changes everything you see.

A profile with no data says so in words: no zeros, no empty charts pretending to be measurements. Having no information and having normal information are two different things, and the screen tells them apart.

What this system is not

It does not replace professional medical care. In an emergency, call your emergency services.

It does not diagnose, prescribe, or change treatments.

It holds no medical certification or regulatory approval. It is a platform in development, in a pilot phase.
